# Medina Homestays Marketing OS

A custom marketing operations system built for Medina Homestays to organize outreach, partnerships, daily tasks, reporting, and performance tracking in one place.

## Live Demo

https://anany2009.github.io/medina-marketing-os/

## Problem

Medina Homestays needed a more organized way to manage outreach and partnership-building. Without a central system, contact lists, daily follow-ups, reports, and performance tracking could become scattered across messages, spreadsheets, and manual updates.

## What I Built

I built a custom internal marketing operations dashboard that helps the team manage outreach workflows, track progress, assign daily contacts, communicate internally, and generate structured reports. The system was designed for a small non-technical team to use consistently without needing manual supervision for every task.

## Key Features

- Database of 1,598 Islamic organizations across 16+ countries
- Daily outreach workflow for assigning new contacts
- Contact tracking for mosques, Islamic centers, schools, nonprofits, and community organizations
- Quick access to email, WhatsApp, websites, and follow-up actions
- KPI dashboard for tracking completed tasks, outreach progress, and team activity
- Daily and weekly reporting workflows
- Internal messaging between team members and administration
- Role-based views for team members and administrators
- Firebase-backed data storage and real-time updates
